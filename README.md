## Two tracks that run every single week

**Distribution — 30 min/day, from Week 2.** Customer conversations, writing in public,
market contact. Target: 30 conversations by Week 8. This is the block that decides whether
this repo becomes a portfolio or a company.

**Coding agents.** Use them for boilerplate, tests, refactors, and review. Never for
architecture you can't explain, and never for the Week 9 agent loop — write that one
yourself. Log the prompts that saved real time.

## The bar for every week

- A repo artifact. A watched course does not count.
- README, architecture note, tests, and at least one documented failure mode.
- Deployed and reachable (from Week 2 onward).
- Cost and latency measured, not guessed (from Week 4 onward).
